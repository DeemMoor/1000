# Card decks

Both decks are by Dmitry Fomin (Дмитрий Фомин), released under CC0 1.0
(public domain dedication), downloaded from Wikimedia Commons:

- `atlas/` — Atlasnye (Атласная) deck, after Adolf Charlemagne's 1862 design:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:SVG_Atlasnye_playing_cards
- `english/` — English pattern deck:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:SVG_English_pattern_playing_cards
  Its `back.svg` is the green and dark red back from the Atlas deck.

Files are named by card id: rank (`9 10 J Q K A`) + suit (`S C D H`).
